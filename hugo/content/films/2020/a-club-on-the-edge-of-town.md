Brett Chapman returns to Leeds theatre company Slung Low, following his film ‘Standing In The Rain’ (LIFF33). Based in Holbeck Working Men’s Club, possibly the oldest club in the UK, Slung Low responds to the current pandemic by acting on its core values, in support of the community it serves.
