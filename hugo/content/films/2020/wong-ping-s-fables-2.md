Fresh from his UK debut solo exhibition last year, we present one of Hong Kong artist Wong Ping’s latest animations. Weird it may be, but it retains a fascinatingly unique internal logic. Touching on consumerism, corruption and the police state, its retro-video-game-pop style exposes a refreshing subversiveness.
