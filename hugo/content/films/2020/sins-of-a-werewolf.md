Sins of a Werewolf is a darkly comic, tongue-in-cheek send-up of the Catholic church, in the vein of Father Ted, only with more blood and guts. When a seasoned parish priest is bitten on the arse by a mysterious animal, he begins transforming into a werewolf every full moon. The ensuing bloodshed leads to a resurgence in Mass attendances from fearful locals, much to the delight of his senior colleagues.
