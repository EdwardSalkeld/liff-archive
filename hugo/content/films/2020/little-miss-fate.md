Towering above us, the great hand of fate is playing the tunes of our lives. But everyone needs a break once in a while. What is unexpected however, is the chaos and debauchery unleashed when a few small innocent interventions are made. An extreme satire on love, destiny and chance. Perhaps the most melancholic choice in the programme this year, Mascot shows us the exhausting life of an urban fox who is training to be a professional Mascot. More than that, the film is a sophisticated critique of zombified youth, obsessed with celebrity culture and working dead end jobs.
