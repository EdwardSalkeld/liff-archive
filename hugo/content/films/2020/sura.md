Korean poet Baek Seok once used a spider as a metaphor for family. Hae-Ji Jeong draws on the poem’s meanings and explores the feelings of a college student who is dealing with her friend’s abortion. She delicately exposes how issues of shame and vulnerability affect women in Korean society.
