Aged around 20, Simon has a brain tumour that is gradually destroying his memory and has condemned him to live in the immediate present. From this painful experience, his sister, Nora Štrbová has made an organic short animated documentary, which succeeds with a rare formal relevance to represent the inexorable loss of the being.
