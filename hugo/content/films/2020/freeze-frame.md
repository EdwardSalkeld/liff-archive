Filmmaking is essentially a process of freezing time, and animator Soetkin Verstegen investigates this concept with a superb stop motion short. As workers manufacture blocks of ice in cold wilderness, life becomes trapped within its own repetitive cycle. It speaks to the nature of time, memory and animated film itself.
