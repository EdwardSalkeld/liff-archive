Created as part of the series ‘Present Tense’, this short is a spoken word/documentary hybrid, that poetically reflects on personal experiences of the British education system. Debris Stevenson returns to her school memories, relating her feelings as someone with dyslexia, and the frustration of being labelled a failure.
