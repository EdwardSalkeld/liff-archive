The record plays, but the party’s over. Baggage carousels and supermarket conveyors trundle aimlessly. Disco balls spin and water sprinklers sprinkle. Where has everyone gone? Set to Beethoven’s Piano Sonata No. 14, animator Geoffroy de Crécy presents us with a world that continues on, but without humanity to witness it.
