Maud is coming to terms with the death of her abusive mother, refusing visitors and confining herself to her house. When Maud’s prayer beads go missing, she is happy to dismiss the disappearance but as the strange happenings go from bad to worse, Maud is left with no choice but to confront the spirit hiding in the shadows of her home.
