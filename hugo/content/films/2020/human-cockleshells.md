Accompanying a song by music collective Elephantstopper, Human Cockleshells relates the story of a lonely lighthouse keeper and the sea creatures that join him, sharing a helpless cry for meaning in their lives. This animation is a sad and funny satire centred around an impressively stuttering guitar solo. That’s enough.
