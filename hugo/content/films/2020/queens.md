An enthralling and almost hypnotic insight into the stream of consciousness of a man’s hazy descent through the nightmarish streets of London in search of ‘luck’, which encapsulates both the instantly recognisable realities of the urban landscape along with the fast-paced and erratic internal thoughts of the intoxicated narrator. The story follows Michael, a quiet man not used to grand gestures of emotion, as he embarks on an extraordinary journey one evening after work. By following his heart, Michael finds himself part of the drag community and his understanding of family will change forever.
