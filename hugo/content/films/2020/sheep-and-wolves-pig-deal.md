The united village of sheep and wolves leads its steady and peaceful life
until two unexpected guests suddenly arrive. Hot on their heels comes a
pack of wild wolves who plan to destroy the village and eat the sheep.
Grey, the new leader of the united tribe, now has to convince the sheep
and wolves that you can deal with any big problems or serious challenges
only by working together – because power is in unity! A sequel to the
equally entertaining Sheep and Wolves (LYFF 2019).

> ‘It’s important that a film is made about Alzheimer’s disease, as the subject is still taboo. People are ashamed of this disease, afraid that they will be considered ‘crazy’. If we break the taboo and talk about it more as a disease then a lot of people will feel less lonely. It’s just a part of life.’

Director, Mischa Kamp
