A sudden meeting of a Man and a Woman begins a hypnotic act of pleasure and discomfort. They slowly get closer to each other in a game of mutual seduction and unclothing. III is a portrait of a woman staying in an uneasy and exhausting relationship with a man, which allures her and repulses her at the same time.
