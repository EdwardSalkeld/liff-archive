An enthralling and almost hypnotic insight into the stream of consciousness of a man’s hazy descent through the nightmarish streets of London in search of ‘luck’, which encapsulates both the instantly recognisable realities of the urban landscape along with the fast-paced and erratic internal thoughts of the intoxicated narrator.
