Doggerland follows Musa Nasir, an illegal immigrant and refugee, scraping an existence working at an exploitative UK car wash. Homeless, underpaid, Musa is determined to turn his life around and make it in the UK. In an attempt to escape his grim reality, he makes a drastic decision and takes on an underground driving job.
