Filmed in West Cork and exclusively using local, non-actors, Ballast follows Michael, as he grieves for the death of his child. Beautifully composed Director of Photography highlights the small touching moments of Michael’s life, as he withdraws from the world, to construct a way of leaving this earth behind.
