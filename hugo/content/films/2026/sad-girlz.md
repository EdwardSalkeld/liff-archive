Fernanda Tovar’s award-winning debut is an intimate, deeply empathetic portrait of female friendship in the aftermath of trauma.

Paula and La Maestra are inseparable friends and star swimmers until an incident at a party tests their bond as they struggle with differing responses to what happened. With exceptional performances from Rocío Guzmán and Darana Álvarez, this sensitive coming-of-age drama explores how young women navigate sexual violence, friendship and the possibility of finding joy again.

> "A sensitive and knowing study of female teenage friendship, powered by strong performances from its young leads." — Nikki Baughan, Screen Daily

**Content Guidance:** Contains themes of sexual violence and trauma.
