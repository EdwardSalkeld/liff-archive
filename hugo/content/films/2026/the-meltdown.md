Manuela Martelli’s second feature is an intense thriller set in 90s Chile, centering on a missing teenager in a remote ski resort.

9-year-old Ines is used to seeing people come and go at her family’s resort and when young German skier Hanna arrives, they form a fast friendship. But when Hanna goes missing, Ines observes the reaction of her community without truly understanding. A gripping film about the tensions of a small community as their country undergoes political transition.

> "Confused connections, parental proxies, colonial histories -- Martelli interweaves these narrative strands with skill and artistry." — Sheri Linden, Hollywood Reporter

**Content Guidance:** Contains themes of grief and trauma.
