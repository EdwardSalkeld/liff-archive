## SHORT FILMS IN THIS PROGRAMME

Please note this short film programme contains flashing images.

## Caeneus

Captivated by Kaineus' performance on a punk stage, Kainis is consumed by an obsession to possess his body. Through a nightmarish yet vulnerable fling, this Greek-myth retelling unveils the complexity of desire, gender envy, and queer transformation. UK Premiere | August Joensalo | Finland | 2026 | 15 mins | Finnish

## Typhoon Mambo

A widow finds solace in an AI chatbot that lets her converse with her dead husband, until his former caretaker returns and unsettles the fragile refuge she built. Typhoon Mambo delicately probes the shifting boundaries between grief, memory and the future. UK Premiere | Domenico Singha Pedroli | France, Switzerland | 2026 | 23 mins | Thai

## A Shot At Art

At a renowned annual art festival, two veteran volunteers can’t resist taking a sneak peek at the new, buzzy performance art piece they have been stationed outside of. Inside, age, gender and power are at war. The weapon of choice? A paintball gun. Yorkshire Premiere | Ilke Paddenburg | Netherlands | 2025 | 17 mins | Dutch

## SLAP!!

In a desperate search for acceptance, a troubled young man takes part in brutal, bloody competitions. Disregarding the consequences, he increasingly negotiates the limits of what he is willing to do to gain adoration. Mikolaj Piszczan | Poland | 2025 | 22 mins | Polish

## Faux Bijoux

In Lebanon, Mireille promises Johnny a shot at the big screen. But this audition hides a darker bargain: dreams of stardom, escape and a better life collide with the brutal dynamics of illegal surrogacy, migration, and a visa that could change everything. Yorkshire Premiere | Jessy Moussallem | France, Lebanon | 2026 | 22 mins | Arabic, English, Finnish

**Content Guidance:** Contains scenes with nudity and graphic violence.
