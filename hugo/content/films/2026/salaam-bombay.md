Mira Nair’s extraordinary debut plunges us into the teeming streets of Bombay through the eyes of ten-year-old Krishna.

Abandoned by his family, he joins a community of children surviving on the margins of the city, determined to earn enough money to return home. Shot on location with a largely non-professional cast, Salaam Bombay! combines neorealist immediacy with deep compassion in its heartbreaking portrait of childhood and survival.

> "A film of unflinching realism that offers no catharsis, and also of thrilling vitality that allows no pity." — Devika Girish, Criterion

**Content Guidance:** Contains scenes of drug use and themes of child exploitation, sexual exploitation and poverty.
