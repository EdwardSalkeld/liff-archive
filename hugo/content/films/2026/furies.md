A riveting heist thriller about two sisters fighting back against Lebanon’s real-life corruption-fuelled economic collapse when banks froze accounts overnight, trapping millions of people’s life savings.

Maro, a determined single mother, tries to access her money to pay for her wild-hearted younger sister Dalia’s life-saving surgery. When the bank refuses, the sisters plan an elaborate all-night heist to reclaim what is rightfully theirs, triggering out of control events in a city already on the brink.
