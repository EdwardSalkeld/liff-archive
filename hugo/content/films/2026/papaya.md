A tiny papaya seed rebels against becoming a tree in this stunning Brazilian animated debut from Priscilla Kellen, dialogue-free and with an upbeat score from Talita Del Collado.

Little Papaya has always been a curious being, with a dream of flying and need for freedom. Realising she will soon have to root, she must fight against nature and keep moving, in this joyous, dialogue-free adventure about discovering the power within you. Accompanied by a soundtrack of Brazilian beats, Papaya is an imaginative and psychedelic experience for all ages.

> "A charming animated debut that captures audiences due to it's gorgeous 2D animation and the lively score that serenades the titular seedling's journey." — Giovanni Lago, Next Best Picture
