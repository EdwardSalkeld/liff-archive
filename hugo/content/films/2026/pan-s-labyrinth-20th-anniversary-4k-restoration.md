Guillermo del Toro’s beloved masterpiece is meticulously restored in 4K from the original 35mm negative.

In post-Civil War Spain in 1944, Ofelia moves with her pregnant mother to live with her new stepfather, a brutal captain in Franco’s army. She discovers a mysterious labyrinth, where a faun tells her she must complete three dangerous tasks to reclaim her throne in the underworld. As Ofelia navigates the trials, the lines between her harsh reality and the fantastical world blur.
