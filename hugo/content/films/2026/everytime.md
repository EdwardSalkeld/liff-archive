One of the most acclaimed films of the year, Everytime is the beguiling third feature from Austrian writer-director Sandra Wollner who has crafted an elusive and dreamlike portrait of grief.

A mother, daughter and teenage boy are brought together by tragedy. They travel to Tenerife to escape the rhythms of daily life, but under the watchful gaze of the summer heat, the trip becomes a haunting space where the boundaries of memory, reality and reconciliation blur.

Please note this film contains flashing images.

> "A sun-dappled film about death and love that might be the best in Cannes this year." — Rory O'Connor, The Film Stage
