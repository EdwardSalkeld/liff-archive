Playtime is Jacques Tati’s gloriously inventive satire of modernity, and a portrait of a city in motion.

As the bewildered Monsieur Hulot navigates a futuristic Paris of glass, gadgets and labyrinthine offices, he becomes entangled with a group of equally disoriented characters. Almost entirely wordless and packed with meticulously choreographed visual humour, Tati’s expansive widescreen spectacle turns the modern world into an absurd playground.

> "For my money Playtime is the greatest film ever made." — David Jenkins, Little White Lies
