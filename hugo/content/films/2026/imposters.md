A couple is put to the test when their missing baby returns under mysterious circumstances in this chilling lo-fi sci-fi.

Paul and Marie are a picture-perfect couple whose lives are shattered when their baby, Theo, is taken from them. But when Marie returns with an unharmed Theo sometime later, Paul suspects the child isn’t theirs and that something dark is at play. Caleb Phillips crafts a smart, tense, and at times harrowing film centred around a stellar performance from Jessica Rothe.

> "Caleb Phillips establishes himself as an audacious new voice in genre by utilizing a high-concept mystery, complete with shocking twists, to drive a tense, creative exploration of an overused plot device that feels original." — Sara Clements, Next Best Picture

**Content Guidance:** Contains distressing scenes of parental grief and strong violence.
