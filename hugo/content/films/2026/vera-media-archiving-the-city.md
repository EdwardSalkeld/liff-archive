Yorkshire and North East Film Archive presents a special screening of archive film from Leeds including spotlighting the remarkable work of Vera Media, the pioneering Leeds-based women's film and television collective founded in 1985.

Renowned for its award-winning documentaries and dramas, Vera Media explored social issues, the arts, education and community life. This unique showcase offers a retrospective on the city's people, places and stories from the 1980s to the early 2000s. The films will be presented by introductions from the Yorkshire and North East Film Archive.
