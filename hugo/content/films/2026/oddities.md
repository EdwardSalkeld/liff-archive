A mixture of thriller and horror with touches of comedy, featuring a magnificent Adrienne Barbeau claiming her reign as horror queen.

When two self-destructive best friends accidentally kill their drug dealer, they have to flee town. Stopping at a roadside antique shop, they quickly discover that the older woman running the place isn’t as sweet as she seems. It’s out of the frying pan and into the fire as they fight for survival while the criminals they were running away from descend on the property…

> " The core cast is small, but they work perfectly. When I thought I knew where the story was headed, I was never quite right. And I loved that about it." — Karina Adelgaard, Heaven of Horror
