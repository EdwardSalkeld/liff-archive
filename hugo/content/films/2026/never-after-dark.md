A travelling medium uncovers dark, threatening secrets in this spine-chilling ghost story.

Airi helps lost souls cross over to the afterlife. Arriving at a remote location, she encounters a spirit that will only come out at night and that refuses to leave. With no other option, Airi breaks her own rules to try and understand the spirit’s strange behaviour and immediately puts herself in danger. Dave Boyle’s masterful storytelling gives us a fresh and terrifying take on the haunted house genre.

> "A terrific slow-burn horror film that evolves from a supernatural ghost story into something darker, bloodier and surprisingly emotional. Moeka Hoshi is excellent, the atmosphere is haunting, and the final act makes the patient build worth it." — Elliott Collins, Movie Files
