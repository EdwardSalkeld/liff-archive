Henry Chaisson’s atmospheric debut set in a Gothic world is a claustrophobic descent into horror.

After receiving a mysterious phone call, sound engineer Joan (Sasha Frolova) returns to her father’s New England estate in the aftermath of a devastating fire, where her painter father lies in bed with severe burns, watched over by the crossbow-wielding housekeeper. Joan must trace a series of ghostly audio fragments to confront the unearthed demons of her family’s past.

> "Recluse hits all the tropes that one expects to find in this type of horror film, yet it manages to push this story in a daring, disturbing new direction that uses sound as a superpower. It’s a hell of a directorial debut from Henry Chaisson." — Daniel Kurland, Bloody Disgusting

**Content Guidance:** Contains scenes with strong violence and domestic abuse.
