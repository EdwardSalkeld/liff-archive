Five friends must find their way out of an abandoned tower in this tense horror-thriller.

Set to get to the bottom of the urban legend born out of a string of mysterious disappearances in the sealed tower of a local housing project, a group of friends embark on a night expedition inside the building. They’re soon chased by something or someone who has no intention of letting them out alive, and as they try and find a way out, every door opened is a new terror awaiting them.

> "This French debut is a relentless, white-knuckle thrill ride." — Trace Thurman, Bloody Disgusting
