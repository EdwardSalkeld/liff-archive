A fresh and joyful documentary which is a true ode to DIY, friendship and music with a generous dose of punk spirit

Described as the most punk rock band in Britain, Ultimate Thunder are musicians with learning disabilities, Down syndrome and autism based in Leeds. The film follows their story over three years as they develop new music and play to increasingly larger crowds, all while facing challenges including cuts to arts funding, major surgery and the lead singer losing his voice.

> "A powerful new documentary telling the story of one of Leeds’ most unique and resilient musical forces" — Benjamin Jackson, Yorkshire Evening Post
