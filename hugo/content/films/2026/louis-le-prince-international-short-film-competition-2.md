## SHORT FILMS IN THIS PROGRAMME

## Burn and fade

Set amongst a forest full of ghosts, two septuagenarians share their reflections on life, love, and belonging, during an escape from their retirement home. UK Premiere | David Arslanian | France | 2025 | 15 mins | French Sign Language

## Nobody Barks

When Candela accidentally runs over her nephew's dog, she avoids confession by inventing a legend about a dog pilgrimage to a sacred tree. As the lie begins to consume her, this deadpan, absurdist look at folklore asks how far we'll go to protect ourselves. Yorkshire Premiere | Júlia Coldwell Serra | Spain | 2025 | 20 mins | Catalan

## Surface Tension

When competitive swimmer Bulet returns to her hometown in Laguna for a wedding, she finds herself drowning out of water. Blending archival photos and underwater shots, this retelling of the siren myth explores the inseparable losses of love, land, and lineage. UK Premiere | The Serrano Sisters | Philippines | 2025 | 17 mins | Tagalog

## As a Cure

Sifting through expansive sand dunes, a girl searches for her identity card to pursue personal artistic desires, a decision that would create a seismic shift within her family and future. UK Premiere | Dorothea Sing Zhang | China, UK | 2026 | 17 mins | Mandarin Chinese

## The Spectacular Death of Prisca

On her daughter’s wedding day, an overprotective mother finds herself catapulted into the chaotic world of an old Nollywood film. As she embarks on a mission to rescue her daughter, what unfolds is a high-energy, entertaining love letter to Nollywood cinema. European Premiere | Kaelo Iyizoba | Nigeria | 2026 | 23 mins | English

**Content Guidance:** Contains discussion of discrimination.
