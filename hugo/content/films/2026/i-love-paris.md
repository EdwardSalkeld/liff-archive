Deadpan mockumentary about an up-and-coming musician whose career ambitions are complicated when she is turned into a bloodthirsty creature of the night.

Paris is desperate to get her music career off the ground. But after an attempt to film a TikTok results in her becoming a vampire, she refuses to let her new nocturnal lifestyle, or the lack of daytime gigs, get in the way. Instead, she begins to realise that her newfound powers might actually help her achieve her dreams.

> "A delightfully twisted mockumentary that blends vampire mythology with influencer satire to wickedly funny effect." — Dan Tabor, Cinapse
