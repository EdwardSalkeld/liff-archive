Acclaimed for his short films - Pale Saint screened in the British short film competition at LIFF 2019 - writer-director Rhys Marc Jones has made a striking debut feature with Black Church Bay, a gripping and thought-provoking mystery thriller.

In a Welsh fishing village, deputy head teacher Barry (Tom Cullen) starts an affair with one of his students Jasper (Joe Locke). One night, Jasper mysteriously disappears, and Barry’s closely guarded secret threatens to come to light.

**Content Guidance:** Contains scenes with nudity and sex.
