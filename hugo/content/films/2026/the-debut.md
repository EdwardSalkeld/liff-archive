Actor-writer-director Jesse Eisenberg returns after A Real Pain (LIFF 2024) with a lovingly crafted comedy set in the world of theatre.

When Mona Friedman (Julianne Moore) is cast in a bit part at a small community theatre, she transforms from a shy, unassuming housewife into a zealous method actor willing to do anything to protect the artistic integrity of her marginal role - even if it means waging war against the show’s domineering director (Paul Giamatti).

> “If ever there was an instant classic example of comedy timing and delivery it is what Moore does here. Priceless.” — Pete Hammond, Deadline
