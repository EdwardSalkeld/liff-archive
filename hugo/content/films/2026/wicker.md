Olivia Colman’s perfect wicker husband becomes the source of much jealousy in this charming one-of-a-kind romance.

Fisherwoman is often ridiculed by her peers for being unmarried – that is until she orders herself an unusual but perfect husband who sends the village into a frenzy. With an all-star cast including (former Leeds student) Alexander Skarsgard as the man made of wicker, Alex Huston Fischer and Eleanor Wilson’s film is a funny, poetic, and a-bit-out-there tale of how love makes the world go round.

> "A warming, sometimes poignant pleasure, a film full of lively personality and possessed of a rather humane outlook on our petty foibles." — Richard Lawson, The Hollywood Reporter
