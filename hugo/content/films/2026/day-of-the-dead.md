Featuring some of the most acclaimed horror films of the year, Day of the Dead on Halloween opens with Never After Dark, a spine-chilling ghost story from Japan. Two films from the USA follow – creepy Gothic horror Recluse and unsettling genre-bending mystery Imposters. The day’s finale is the wild martial arts horror comedy Ghost in the Cell from Indonesia

## FILMS

- Never After Dark
- Recluse
- Imposters
- Ghost in the Cell

Passes for the whole day are available. To buy tickets for individual films instead, please book on the film pages.
