A raunchy but heartfelt queer spoof about a viral outbreak which is turning gay men straight. Lucien is a timid twink who idolises gay social media influencer Jim Queen (and his rippling abs).

When a mysterious virus begins turning proud gay men into cliché heterosexuals, Lucien must team up with his hero (who has contracted the disease) and journey through the edges of queer culture to find a cure and save the LGBTQIA+ community in this rapid-fire comedy from France.

> "It’s an animated charmer that blends raunch and warmth into a memorable gem." — Zachary Lee, RogerEbert.com
