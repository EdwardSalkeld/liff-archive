Rudi Rosenberg’s warm, funny and heartbreaking Words of Love features captivating performances from new young actors Nour Salam and Mateo Danila, the brilliant Hafsia Herzi, and a mudi shepherd as a cheese-obsessed stray dog.

Erika is the anchor of a household that rarely stands still, doing her best to raise teenage Abigail, and her whirlwind little brother, Yoni. When Abi hears that her long-absent father has been seen nearby, Erika fears the fragile family balance will collapse for good.

> "A classic melodrama centred on its main characters, deliberately modest yet highly effective, shot in a realism beautifully captured by cinematographer Éric Dumont." — Fabien Lemercier, Cineuropa
