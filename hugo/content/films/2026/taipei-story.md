Edward Yang’s melancholy masterpiece captures Taipei at a moment of dizzying transformation.

As ambitious Chin looks towards the future, her boyfriend Lung, played by fellow New Taiwan Cinema master Hou Hsiao-hsien, remains tethered to the past, and their relationship begins to drift apart. Against a cityscape of gleaming towers and neon-lit streets, Yang’s gorgeous compositions capture the pace of urban change while finding moments of intimacy and beauty within the modern metropolis.

> "So much of the pleasure of watching the film is rooted in its masterful application of light, shadow, and color." — Angeline Gragasin, Screen Slate

**Content Guidance:** Contains scenes of violence.
