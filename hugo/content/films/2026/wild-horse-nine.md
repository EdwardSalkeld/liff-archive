From writer-director Martin McDonagh (The Banshees of Inisherin), Wild Horse Nine is a gripping dark comedy thriller set shortly before the 1973 coup in Chile.

CIA agents Chris (John Malkovich) and Lee (Sam Rockwell) are dispatched from Santiago to Easter Island by their bureau chief, MJ (Steve Buscemi). Amongst the Island's iconic statues, Chris's new bond with a pair of rebellious students (Mariana di Girolamo and Ailín Salas) threatens to upend everyone’s trip to this remote paradise.

> "A bittersweet hangout film about lonely, crumpled men facing up to mortality, dealing with the consequences of violence and chewing the fat to outlandishly funny effect." — Philip De Semlyen, Time Out

**Content Guidance:** Contains scenes of strong violence.
