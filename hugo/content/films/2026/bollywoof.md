A Yorkshire dog, voiced by David Jonsson (the new Black Panther, Rye Lane ), has his life upended when his family move to Mumbai in this all-star animation from the team behind The Amazing Maurice.

After Basil’s pawrent (Olivia Colman) is relocated for work, Basil and his favourite human Luke are nervous to leave behind their beloved Yorkshire home. When the family arrive in Mumbai and realise Basil has been separated from them, things get worse. Basil's only hope? Befriending a Bollywood obsessed street dog, voiced by Kunal Nayyar (The Big Bang Theory), and a feisty holy cow. Together this unlikely trio embark on an action packed adventure to reunite the family. Presented with Leeds Young Film Festival.

**Content Guidance:** Contains scenes of mild threat.
