The found footage genre meets liminal space horror in Nicholas Pineda's tense and unnerving Infirmary.

On his first shift as a night watchmen, former soldier, Edward encounters increasingly strange phenomena alongside his coworker, retired police officer, Lester. As the unlikely partners navigate the empty halls of an old psychiatric hospital, seemingly unnatural events begin to occur that reveal not only the secrets of the building's past, but of the men themselves.

> "Some clever camera construction and an intensely oppressive atmosphere keep the audience on edge throughout as it throws well executed scares and an intriguing found-footage mystery onto the screen." — Kat Hughes, THN
