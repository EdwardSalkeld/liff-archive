The Fanomenon all-night horror marathon returns to the iconic Hyde Park Picture House – and this year on Friday 13th - with a new mix of midnight horror and cult carnage. Can you survive the night?

This year’s line-up opens with the intense French horror thriller No Way Down, followed by action-horror Oddities about two self-destructive best friends, and creeping dread and terror in Infirmary. The night comes to a gory close with a 4K re-release of From Beyond, a cult masterpiece from horror legend Stuart Gordon, the director of Re-Animator.

Book early to get your favourite seats. Individual tickets are not available for Night of The Dead.

## FEATURES

- No Way Down
- Infirmary
- Oddities
- From Beyond (4K Restoration)
