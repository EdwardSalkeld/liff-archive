A unique collaborative documentary told through the dual perspectives of Adam, a transgender teenager, and his mother, visual artist Amy Jenkins.

Filmed by the pair over two decades, the film offers a distinctive and authentic collage of Adam’s gender transition alongside the everyday challenges of teenage life. Through the films creative and poetic lens, it captures a bold and joyful portrait of identity, friendship and the rhythms of family life.

> "Intimate, expertly-crafted doc celebrates power of family and identity" — Nikki Baughan, Screen Daily
