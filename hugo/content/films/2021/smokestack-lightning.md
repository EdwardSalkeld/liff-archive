An escape from reality, we follow two strangers through a sizzling, quirky outburst of dance in a retro-futuristic world. This short chronicles the adventure of our two heroes as they navigate their shared dreamscape, devoid of time and relieved of convention. A dance film taking you on a 360 degree journey from rural to urban; from the earth’s natural elements to the metropolis humans have created. This years student ‘curtain-raiser’ comes from the Northern Film School.
