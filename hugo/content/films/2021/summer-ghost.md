Have you heard of summer ghosts? The urban legend tells of the ghost
of a young woman who appears when fireworks are lit. Tomoya, Aoi,
and Ryo are high school students who are unable to find their place
in the world. Each has their own reasons for meeting the ghost, and
one warm summer night, when life and death intersect, where will their
emotions take them? Summer Ghost is the directorial debut of loundraw,
who previously illustrated I Want to Eat Your Pancreas and Josee,
The Tiger and the Fish.
