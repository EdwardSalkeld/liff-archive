Street art seen on the walls of Valencia evolves into a fast paced animated documentary. María Lorenzo Hernández started collecting images from the city’s streets using her mobile phone, describing them as ‘hidden treasure’. Gabriel de Paco, who wrote the soundtrack, was also discovered playing in the streets of Valencia.
