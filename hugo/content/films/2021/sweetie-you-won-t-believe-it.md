Days before his wife is due to give birth, Dastan decides to shirk his
responsibilities and go on a fishing trip with his two best, if not very
bright, friends. Things are going well until the hapless trio witness a
gangland execution and are pursued by thugs who want to silence
them. As things spiral out of control this masterpiece of horror comedy
delivers a series of ever more ridiculous and gory scenes while still
managing to deal with the themes of masculinity, fatherhood and
friendship.

> ‘I was inspired by all the men who like fishing and have families and children, and have to face everyday problems. I wanted to show all these everyday problems in the film but also to present them in a way that seems unreal and ridiculous at the same time. I wanted the film to be both funny and horrific. I met a friend whom I have not seen for years and he invited me to a place far from the city, in the countryside, which attracts many fishermen. So, when I was writing the script, I remembered the place and I felt it was appropriate for this movie, as it also fit the nature of the characters.’

Director Ernar Nurgaliev, from an interview with Asian Movie Pulse.
