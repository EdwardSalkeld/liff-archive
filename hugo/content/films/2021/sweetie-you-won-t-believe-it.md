Days before his wife is due to give birth, Dastan decides to shirk his
responsibilities and go on a fishing trip with his two best, if not very
bright, friends. Things are going well until the hapless trio witness a
gangland execution and are pursued by thugs who want to silence
them. As things spiral out of control this masterpiece of horror comedy
delivers a series of ever more ridiculous and gory scenes while still
managing to deal with the themes of masculinity, fatherhood and
friendship.
