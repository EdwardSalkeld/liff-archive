A spellbinding cinematic collage of hundreds of films from around
the world brings together an ensemble cast of actors with one thing
in common: each is no longer alive. Together, they contend with a
fragile existence lived solely through these traces of their work. The
Afterlight itself exists as a single 35mm film print, further eroding every
time it screens, a poignant reminder of the ephemeral power of cinema.
LIFF presents a unique chance to see this mesmerising film before
it eventually disappears entirely.
