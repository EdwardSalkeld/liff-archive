This short documentary explores the people, practices and moments which make up contemporary rural life of Britain’s village halls, including a thwarted pantomime rehearsal, dreamlike dance displays and a macabre local history presentation. These timeworn structures are the stages upon which local identities, tensions and rituals are all played out.
