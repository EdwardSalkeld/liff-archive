King Vidor’s silent classic The Crowd is a landmark in realist filmmaking
and a lacerating critique of the American dream. John Sims is a young
man who travels to New York City in search of his fortune but ends up
as a corporate drone in the cavernous empire of the Atlas Insurance
Company. He falls in love with a beautiful young woman and they get
married and have kids, but then tragedy strikes and everything starts
to fall apart. Presented by Opera North with a live accompaniment by
pianist Matthew Bourne. This event is ticketed by Opera North. Passes
will not be able to be redeemed against this event. Please visit leedsfilm.
com for more information.
