A young man trapped in a soul-destroying job becomes obsessed with playing an old claw arcade machine in a night market, not realising that the ‘Prize’ he is trying to win is more terrifying than he can imagine. In a small coastal village in the 19th century, a family prepares for the night of “The Truce”, a ritual to ward off evil creatures. Olalla, unlike her parents, does not believe in superstitions. And she doesn’t think They are coming either…
