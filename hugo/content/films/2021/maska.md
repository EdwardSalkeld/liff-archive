11 Preliminary Orbits is the second time the Quay Brothers have tackled Lem’s work. Maska is an adaptation of the author’s 1974 short story about the demonic female robot, Duenna, who has to annihilate the rebel threatening the royal throne. Accompanied by the composition De Natura Sonoris by Krzysztof Penderecki. Roly Poly is a short film by one of Poland’s most influential directors, Andrzej Wajda, adapting another Lem story about a rally driver who survives a series of terrible accidents thanks to a brilliant surgeon who transplants multiple body parts, with complex ethical repercussions. A prescient parable of the near future.
