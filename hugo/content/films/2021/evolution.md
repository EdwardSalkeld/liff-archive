Hungarian director Kornél Mundruczó and screenwriter Kata
Wéber (White God and Pieces of a Woman) return with a powerful
and richly cinematic drama tracing the historical trauma of the
Second World War through three generations of a family. The film
begins with an extraordinary, wordless sequence with dizzying
cinematography, probing a surreal memory at the liberation of a
concentration camp. Sequences in modern Europe depict a holocaust
survivor in Budapest and a young couple involved in an anti-Semitic
episode in contemporary Berlin as the film follows a trajectory
of pain through to a more hopeful future.
