Petra and her best friend pass a rainy afternoon by trading questions over pistachios. Until one question comes up that she doesn’t know if she can answer… The owner of a gay video store in Iran struggles to figure out if his boyfriend has left him, or if something terrible has happened. With experimental horror vibes, this short puts the viewer in some uncomfortably claustrophobic points of view, leaving the audience also wondering what has gone wrong.
