Surrealist scenes of domestic life that are disorienting and compelling. Produced at the Documentary & Experimental Film Center in Tehran, emerging director Mahboobeh Kalaee has mixed animated objects with hand painted illustrations and filmed them using a roving camera. An innovative method that brilliantly blurs the lines between fiction and reality.
