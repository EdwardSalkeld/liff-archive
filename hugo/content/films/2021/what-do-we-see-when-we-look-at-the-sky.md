In this seductively original urban fairytale, director Alexandre
Koberidze starts with a simple love story and builds a quirkily
unpredictable portrait of life in the beautiful Georgian city of Kutaisi.
For Lisa and Giorgi, it’s love at first sight but although they agree to meet
the next day, they are struck by a curse so they can no longer recognise
each other. Life goes on as usual in their hometown, street dogs stray,
the football world cup begins and a film crew on its quest to find true
love might be what they need.
