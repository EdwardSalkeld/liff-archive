Following a failed crowd-funding attempt to launch their horror-themed
board game, three friends are excited to be invited to meet with
V.V.Stubbs, a famous games designer who has promised to invest in
their idea. When Stubbs dies in a freak accident they realise how much
it looks like murder. With a dead body on their hands, they turn to the
game they created to get rid of the body but can they really ‘Murder
Bury Win’? Michael Lovan’s debut feature is bloody good fun and a
perfect addition to Night of the Dead. Screening with Good Girl (Dean
Hewison, New Zealand, 2021, 4 mins).
