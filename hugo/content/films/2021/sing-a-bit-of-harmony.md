Written and directed by Yasuhiro Yoshiura, who also directed the
brilliant Patema Inverted (LIFF 2013), Sing a Bit of Harmony tells the
story of the beautiful and mysterious Shion, who transfers to Keibu High
School, where she quickly becomes popular due to her open-hearted
personality and exceptional athletic talent. Shion’s goal is to bring
chronic loner Satomi happiness, with her earnestness and classroom
serenades. But her bewildering antics end up involving them all in
some serious trouble. Prepare for the heartwarming story of a not
quite market-ready AI and her classmates!
