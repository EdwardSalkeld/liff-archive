In the north-east of Brazil, the country’s first manned spaceship is about to take flight. Meanwhile, a nearby family also dream of new horizons. A portrait of day-to-day childhood featuring a village, a family, a boy who likes to wear dresses, the return of a wolf and a series of hidden desires.
