Made entirely at home during lockdown, this stop-motion animation takes a look at queer history with the help of hand-made puppets. A touching story about love, loss, and chosen family. Follow Brogues and Sparkly Toes on their adventures of love, loss, dancing, and lots of mud! A tale told entirely through feet.
