Vying for a place in the pantheon of great roller skating movies (yes, that is a thing…), Yorkshire- based artist Vincent James’ wheel-spinning meet cute is a painstakingly animated stop-motion love story, made in Centre Vale Park, Todmorden. Thrill! at the stripey coloured socks! Swoon! at the ‘will they – won’t they?’
