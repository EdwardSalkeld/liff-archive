Pursued by loan sharks, Aoyama is rescued by a mysterious man in
yellow overalls, who offers him the opportunity of a better life in an
isolated, unnamed town - a perpetual holiday resort, controlled by
airport-style security, sonic defences, and a code of conduct called
“The Bible”. Part dystopian conspiracy thriller, part prescient societal
warning, Shinji Araki’s stylised debut intelligently dissects society’s
compulsion for instant gratification aand corporately-controlled
decision-making. Do we want to live in a world where every need is
catered for, but that’s also a prison of inauthentic morality?
