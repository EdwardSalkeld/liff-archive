At Beijing’s Democracy Wall in 1978, the “Stars”, a group of self-
taught artists (including a young Ai Weiwei) challenged propaganda
by showing provocative new art that championed free expression,
often exposing the inhumanity of the Cultural Revolution. Beijing Spring
recounts the experiences of artists and activists who flourished in this
brief period of reform, before crackdowns saw many imprisoned or
exiled. Recording it all was a daring young filmmaker, Chi Xiaoning,
whose astonishing, long-hidden 16mm film footage is revealed here
for the first time in 40 years.
