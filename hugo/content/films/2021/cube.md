A group of strangers awaken in a mysterious square room, with hatches
in each of the walls. Not knowing how or why they are there they begin
to look for an exit, travelling through a series of identical, interconnected
rooms. Soon however it becomes clear that some rooms contain lethal
traps and as the group become more tired and desperate, tempers
flare and their frustrations turn on each other. This remake of Vincenzo
Natali’s claustrophobic sci-fi has a more industrial look and feel with
subtle changes to the original story that provide a modern twist about
the nature of society.
