Three loser brothers (including a caucasian kid, who doesn’t appear to
really know Japanese), are struggling to be popular. But luckily they get
a chance to have a co-ed picnic with some pretty young ladies! And
that’s all the plot you’ll need, or indeed get! Much like flipping through
Japanese TV channels, this audience favourite (LIFF 2006), ranges from
slapstick comedy and weird-sci-fi animation, to Cronenberg-esque
squirty alien creatures and a delightful dance-battle dream sequence.
It’s a brilliantly outrageous collection of 21 deeply surreal episodes
that’ll melt logic, and likely minds.
