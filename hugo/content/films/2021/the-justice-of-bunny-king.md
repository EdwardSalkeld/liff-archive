Bunny King is a headstrong mother of two with a sketchy past and a
razor-sharp tongue. She spends her days washing car windscreens
in order to save enough money to regain custody of her kids. After
promising her daughter a birthday party, Bunny must fight the social
services and break the rules to keep her word, but in doing so risks
losing her children altogether. Accompanied by her niece Tonya, Bunny
is in a race against the clock and headed towards an epic showdown
with the authorities. Gaysorn Thavat’s loveable feature debut is both
a funny and touching social realist drama.
