Set in the perhaps unlikely world of Hong Kong’s astronomically
priced housing market, Coffin Homes plays fast and loose with its
satire, as auteur Fruit Chan conducts a swirling chaos of genres,
involving haunted apartments, a truculent but playful ghost child,
hilariously extravagant splatter horror, rotten estate agents, landlords,
gangs, and even an appearance by the underworld’s Ghost King.
With storylines as compartmentalised and disordered as the tenants’
cramped cubicles, Chan still maintains his interest in social realist
depictions of Hong Kong’s grassroots life and anxieties over the
city’s future.
