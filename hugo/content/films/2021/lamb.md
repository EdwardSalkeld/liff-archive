Manimal freakishness is in plain, matter-of-fact sight in Lamb, a dark
Scandi tall tale evoked with stark clarity. Maria and Ingvar toil away
on their mist-shrouded farm in almost total solitude, a grieving silence
between them long since taken for granted. In the lamb birthing barn,
a miracle arrives, and she is named Ada. Ada is adored, though
perhaps only these isolated and damaged surrogate parents could
accept her for what she is. And as an outsider arrives, we discover
the limits of that acceptance...
