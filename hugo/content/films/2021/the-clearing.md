From first- time student filmmaker, director and writer Daniel Hope, this dark and surreal animation introduces us to Bill (Julian Barratt) who battles in vain to re-woo Deb (Julia Davis) with his below-par outdoor skills and, quite frankly, shocking behaviour, until their last-ditch camping trip takes a very sinister turn.
