Jenny and Tom are excited to install a new Aria “smart security system” in their home. Tom becomes increasingly paranoid about what the system can see, both outside and inside the house. When a group of vicious thieves break into an apartment looking for loot, they don’t reckon with a reclusive engineer at the brink of completing a bizarre, extraordinary machine.
