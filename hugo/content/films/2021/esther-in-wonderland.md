Drawn to the expressive nature of hip-hop and breakdance, Esther tests the boundaries of her restricted existence as a young married woman in a Hasidic community in 1990s Crown Heights. Pedro, working in Switzerland, must take a cow to the slaughterhouse while his mother is dying in Spain.
