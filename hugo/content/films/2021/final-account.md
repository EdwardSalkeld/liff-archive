In 2008, the late British filmmaker Luke Holland began interviewing
the last living generation of Germans to have participated in the Third
Reich, everyday citizens who carried out plans made by the architects
of mass-scale genocide. More than a decade later Holland completed
Final Account, a raw and urgent documentary - featuring in-depth
witness statements, previously unseen archival material, and haunting
current-day footage - that reflects on how otherwise ordinary people
took part in one of the most extraordinary of all human crimes.
