In times of crisis, ordinary people instinctively step up to help each other
in the absence of positive government action. On Our Doorstep tells the
story of a humanitarian crisis at the Jungle camp in Calais, governments
in paralysis, and untrained grassroots civilians attempting to fill the gap.
Creating an intimate patchwork of the experiences of refugees and
volunteers, this film offers a fresh perspective on the continuing refugee
crisis and asks: what is our responsibility in a crisis like this and what are
the limits of citizen action?
