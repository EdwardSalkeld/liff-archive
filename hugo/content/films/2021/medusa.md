21-year-old Mariana belongs to a world where she must keep up the
appearance of a perfect woman. To resist temptation, she and her
girlfriends try to control everything and everyone around them, and that
includes the town sinners. At night, the girl gang put on masks and hunt
down and beat up all women who have deviated from the ‘right’ path.
However, the day will come when the urge to scream will be stronger
than it ever has been. ‘Medusa is wild, glamorous, and marked with
a dark, slick fury.’ (Alliance of Women Film Journalists)
