From Bradford based visual artist Lou Sumray, this beautiful minimalist hand-drawn, experimental animated tale inspired by the antics of the Bowerbird depicts a stark yet enthralling insight into what really happens to all those disposable blue plastic forks that are so recklessly discarded and their impact on the environment.
