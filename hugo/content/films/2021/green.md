Roads have become rivers, and lamp posts abandoned, mysterious totems of a human life disappeared. Relax, calm your senses, and immerse yourself in the gentle rush of water, the chirrup of insects, birdsong and the soft rustling of nature, as a long bucolic evening turns slowly to night. Let... go... In a dreamlike and wordless world, friends Tato and Bere are taking their harvest offerings to the creatures in the forest. They accidentally release a horde of playful characters who are intent on causing havoc, yet their shimmering colours only bring out the beauty in the landscape.
