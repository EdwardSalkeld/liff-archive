Roly Poly is a short film by one of Poland’s most influential directors, Andrzej Wajda, adapting another Lem story about a rally driver who survives a series of terrible accidents thanks to a brilliant surgeon who transplants multiple body parts, with complex ethical repercussions. A prescient parable of the near future.
