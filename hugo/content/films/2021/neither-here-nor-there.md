A personal documentary centering a conversation between the filmmaker and their mother about gender identity. As the middle child of christian pastors, Ley never imagined that transitioning was possible. The film follows Ley´s physical and psychological journey to become themself, while struggling to find a middle ground with their family. A poetic Afrofuturist vision of Black, queer and environmental liberation, inspired by the Parables of Octavia E. Butler.
