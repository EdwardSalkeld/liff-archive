This richly observed feature debut by Norika Sefa follows the quiet
teenager Venera, growing up in a small village in Kosovo. Living in a
crowded, multi-generational household, Venera can rarely find space
or privacy. When Venera befriends the spirited Dorina, a new and
liberating world opens up to her and she begins to push against the
expectations of her conservative patriarchal family. Formally innovative
and beautifully shot, the film makes skillful use of closely cropped
framing to convey the suffocating environment of Venera’s home life,
in this fresh take on the coming-of-age genre.
