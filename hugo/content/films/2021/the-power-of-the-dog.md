Charismatic rancher Phil Burbank (Benedict Cumberbatch) inspires
fear and awe in those around him. When his brother George (Jesse
Plemons) brings home a new wife Rose Gordon (Kirsten Dunst) and her
son Peter (Kodi Smit-McPhee), Phil torments them until he finds himself
exposed to the possibility of love. ‘Jane Campion makes a thrilling
return with The Power of the Dog, a work as boldly idiosyncratic,
unpredictable and alive with psychological complexity as anything
in the revered director’s output.’ (Hollywood Reporter)
