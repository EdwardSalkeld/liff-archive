“Maybe I did something wrong. I probably did do something wrong” says the street sleeper in this harrowing animated documentary. “Just yesterday I was 20... I started limping, I was no use to anybody”. Varya Yakovleva’s ground level composition suggests an ambivalence to homelessness that many will recognise.
