‘... and the life of man, solitary, poor, nasty, brutish, and short’ (Hobbes, Leviathan, 1651). Lines of naked pink creatures wait patiently in line for their turn at the top of a vertiginous rock tower. But this balance gets undermined when one of them whimsically decides to do their own thing.
