From first-time filmmaker Cal Murphy Barton, this documentary tells the surprisingly charming story of a rubber engineer who turns junked scrubber-driers into sex machines, on the site of an abandoned coal mine. An unexpected insight into the remaking of place by combining craft and fetish in a post-industrial landscape.
