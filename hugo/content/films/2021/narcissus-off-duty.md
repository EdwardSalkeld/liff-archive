World famous Brazilian singer-songwriter Caetano Veloso looks
back on his experiences in 1968 when he was arrested and jailed
by the military dictatorship in this quietly fascinating and intimate
documentary. Shot very simply in a bare concrete room without any
distractions, his words carry the film. He is a very moving storyteller,
describing strange encounters with his interrogators and other prisoners
with genuine insight and empathy, speaking openly about painful
memories of enduring solitary confinement and its crushing effect
on the human spirit.
