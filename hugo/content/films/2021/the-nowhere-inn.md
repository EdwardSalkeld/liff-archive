When Carrie Brownstein (Portlandia) sets out to make a documentary
about life on the road with singer-songwriter St Vincent, things take an
endless series of unexpected turns. Annie Clark is the woman behind
St Vincent, and although exotic and flamboyant onstage, her offstage
persona is anything but. A struggle of objectives between Brownstein
and Clark ensues and The Nowhere Inn becomes a film like no other,
a visually striking feast that is in parts comedic mockumentary, concert
film and surreal drama that seeks to interrogate the true essence of
artistic persona.
