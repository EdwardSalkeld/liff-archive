Strange occurrences are afoot in a small seaside town, disturbing Una and her hairy partner’s peaceful lives. Ponies spit, a stranger lurks, anthropomorphic facial growths appear. Director Katarzyna Miechowicz has made a darkly surreal cut-out conundrum that’s as fascinating as it is baffling. Just what are those ponies up to?
