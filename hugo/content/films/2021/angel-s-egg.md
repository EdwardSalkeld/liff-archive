One of the earliest films from Mamoru Oshii (Ghost in the Shell),
Angel’s Egg has an almost mythical status – a mysterious and mystical
anime that audiences rarely get the opportunity to see. A version with
English subtitles was created for LIFF 2009 and we are delighted
to bring back Angel’s Egg as part of our expanded Planet Japan
celebration for 2021. Set in a vast abandoned city, Angel’s Egg follows
the journey of a girl guarding a giant egg and her soldier companion
who tells biblical-like tales.
